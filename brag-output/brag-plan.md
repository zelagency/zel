# Brag Plan: ZEL

> Revision 2: no music (SFX only); message generalised to "hire us and we take your business digital". Scene copy: DIGITAL AGENCY · BANGALORE / FOR YOUR CUSTOMERS "Websites & apps customers love." / FOR YOUR TEAM "Systems your team runs on." with WEBSITE, MOBILE APP, IN-STORE merging into "One dashboard." / ONE AGENCY · FRONT TO BACK "We take your business digital." The storyboard below is the original pass.

## What is this app?
ZEL is a Bangalore agency that builds the storefront customers see *and* the operational systems the team runs on (ordering, kitchen tickets, POS, inventory), on one source of truth.

## The angle
Most agencies ship the website and leave. ZEL's own About page says it plainly: "Every time, the website was the easy half." The reel is a single visual metaphor: **the counter**. Front of house (pale, expressive storefronts) above, back of house (dark, quiet ops screens) below, and ZEL is the one building both. The user asked for 7–9s, so this overrides /brag's 15–25s default.

## Hook (0–1.6s)
Cobalt field, the actual ZEL hero letterforms (SVG paths from `home.html`) snap in with the site's kinetic width-stretch. The brand is identified in under a second.

## Key moments
- Real storefronts tilt in like the site's project cards: Fogstores campaign in a browser frame, BNM Cafe mobile menu in a phone, Infinity Kitchens homepage.
- A dark "back of house" panel rises over them. Three incoming orders labelled WEBSITE / SWIGGY / ZOMATO converge into **one** kitchen ticket. This is the site's own "3→1" ledger claim.

## Outro / punchline
Back to cobalt. A thin line draws across the frame (the counter), and "We build both sides of the counter." lands above the ZEL wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Customer orders on the storefront → the order arrives at the kitchen as one ticket → the team runs it. Shown as storefront screens → order chips merging into a ticket.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with cinematic accents)
- Creative direction: confident agency sizzle, Lusion-grade motion, oversized type
- Interpretation: few words and huge type; fast, eased motion (expo/power4) with real holds on every read. One idea per scene.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support under a polished bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean), excerpt 8.19s→16.19s (starts on a beat), fade out last 0.6s
- Music cue guidance: preset `cues/…vol-12….music-cues.json`, ~110 BPM. Beats relative to excerpt: 0, .55, 1.10, 1.64, 2.18, 2.74, 3.28, 3.83, 4.37, 4.92, 5.45, 6.01, 6.55, 7.09, 7.64. Strong cue 13.11s → **4.92s** (lock the 3→1 merge). Scene cuts snap to 1.64 / 3.83 / 6.01.
- Audio-reactive: subtle; bass drives a soft cobalt glow / panel presence. No visualizers.
- SFX posture: sparse (4–6 cues): soft impact on wordmark, card slides on storefronts, card place on ticket merge, bell on final lockup.
- Restraint: nothing louder than the bed on screen changes; no clicks on every element.

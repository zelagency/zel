# Design
Reference: https://lusion.co/ and supplied a.png through f.png.
Pale lavender #f0f1fa, black ink, cobalt blue #1a2ffb, aqua accent. Five percent desktop margins, fixed 54px pill navigation at top, 2.5vw hero copy, 10vw statement, 8vw section titles. Aeonik with regular weight. 20px media corners. Opening fills one viewport; media begins near 26% of viewport height. Thick animated ribbons sit behind large text. Fogstores is the full-width lead project, using its original fashion photography. Infinity Kitchens and BNM Cafe form the next two-column row, with original brand assets, food photography, and actual app screenshots. On mobile all projects stack. Project detail views show real images and link to live websites. Cabinet Grotesk is limited to the Infinity Kitchens cover, matching its own visual identity. Remaining Lusion reference media is credited.

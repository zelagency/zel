How ZEL started

ZEL began with a pattern: a beautiful website up front, and a business behind it held together by spreadsheets, group chats and dashboards that never agree . So we build both halves: the brand that wins customers, and the systems that deliver every order , on one platform you own. Our goal: a business that works as well as it looks.

HOW WE WORK

Our word,
in writing

  1. §01

    Never: “We’ll hold on to the admin login for now.”

    Everything we make is yours.

    Code, designs, data and accounts sit in your name from day one. Whatever you share with us stays private and protected.

  2. §02

    Never: “Launch has slipped to next month.”

    The date we give is the date we keep.

    Every project starts with a dated plan. If anything puts it at risk, you hear it from us early, never on deadline day.

  3. §03

    Never: “Let me check and get back to you.”

    You’ll never have to chase us.

    A progress update every week and a live preview of the work, so you always know exactly where things stand.

  4. §04

    Never: “That’s going to cost extra.”

    No surprises on the invoice.

    Scope and price are agreed in writing before we start. Anything new is quoted and approved before it is built.
